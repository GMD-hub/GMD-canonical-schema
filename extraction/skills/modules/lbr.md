## Module Code

`MOD-LBR` (Labor)

## Variable Conventions

Labor variables capture employment status, occupation, industry, labor income,
and hours worked. The module covers repeated-period families and dense derivations.

## Expected Variables

- Employment status (employed, unemployed, inactive)
- Occupation codes (ISCO-based)
- Industry codes (ISIC-based)
- Labor income (included — in scope, not welfare)
- Hours worked (usual and reference period)
- Employment sector (public, private, informal)

## Common Edge Cases

- Repeated-period families (e.g., 7-day, 12-month employment)
- Labor income vs. welfare consumption boundary
- ISCO/ISIC version differences across survey years
- Informal employment classification varies
